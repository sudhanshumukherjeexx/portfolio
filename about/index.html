About

I like making data easier to use.

I’m Sudhanshu Mukherjee (সুধাংশু মুখার্জি), a data scientist and AI/ML engineer based in Massachusetts. I enjoy automating processes and getting insight out of data, and I’m happiest where natural language processing, large language models and machine learning meet practical software.

I graduated with a master’s in data science from the University of Massachusetts Dartmouth in May 2025. Before that I spent three years as a BI developer, and since then I’ve worked on multi-agent analytics and built the tools on this site. I’m open to full-time roles and to doctoral research in NLP, multimodal LLMs and machine learning.
